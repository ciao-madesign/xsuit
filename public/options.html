Sites where xsuit always runs

xsuit runs automatically only on these sites. Everywhere else it acts only when you press a button in the popup. Add a site from the popup ("Personalize this site").

    Rules

    Turn off any rule for all sites. Every rule shows how strong its evidence is. The full justification of each rule is in docs/07-evidence-audit.md.

    Hybrid composition (research tools)

    Hybrid is not an automatic mix: you pick exactly which rules it uses. Rules that change the same thing cannot be selected together. The composition itself has no evidence of its own: it is a hypothesis to test.

    Reset

    Privacy

    Everything runs locally. xsuit sends no data anywhere. What it keeps on this device: your settings, and the list of sites where you let it run. Research files are only created when you press Save, after you have seen what they contain.
